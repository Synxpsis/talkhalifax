# TalkHalifax: vision

_Last updated November 3, 2026._

## What it is

TalkHalifax is an always-on local newsroom for the Halifax area. Agents watch official sources around the clock,
turn what's new into short, plain stories, and keep them current. Every story is also a room, where people who care
about it can talk about it live.

The goal is simple: **if something is happening in Halifax that people will care about, it's on TalkHalifax within the
hour, said accurately and plainly, with a place to talk about it.**

## Who it's for

People who live in, work in, or commute through the Halifax Regional Municipality and want to know what affects them
today: road closures, weather, outages, council decisions, housing, transit, things to go to. They read on their phone,
in short moments, and they come back when something big is happening.

## Principles

These come before growth, speed and volume. Every agent and every change is judged against them.

1. **Facts come only from the source.** A story says what the official release says, copied exactly where it matters
   (dates, times, places, amounts). Nothing is invented to fill a gap. Searches, Reddit, other outlets and chat are
   signals of what to look at, never sources of facts.
2. **Honest about time.** Stories are framed against today's date in Halifax. Nobody is invited to something that's over.
3. **Honest about who wrote it.** The journalist is an AI, and says so on every story. It never claims to be human
   and never posts in the chat.
4. **A human holds the pen on what goes out.** New articles and every change to a published story wait for the editor's
   tap on Telegram. Sensitive stories (police, health, people named) always do. The one exception: new numbers on a
   live weather or outage notice (counts and times, same wording) go live at once, and the editor is told after.
5. **Urgent first.** What affects people in the next few hours (P0) leads everything.
6. **Safe rooms.** Chat is screened before anyone sees it, readers can report, and moderators can remove and ban.
   People can delete their data.
7. **Native, quiet, fast.** The site should feel like an app on a phone and a calm reading room on a desktop.
   No ads in the way, no pop-ups, no dark patterns.

## Volume without losing trust

Round-the-clock coverage means many stories a day. Volume is earned by covering more official sources, not by
lowering the bar. A source is added only when it publishes facts we can copy exactly and link back to. If a quieter hour
has nothing new, the right output is nothing.

## Where it's going

| Phase | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Site, live chat per story, sign-in, weather, tides and Getting around | Done |
| 2 | Newsroom MCP, leads queue, hourly journalist, check-ins | Done |
| 3 | Telegram approvals, provincial and RCMP releases, live conditions, search and Reddit signals, profiles | Done |
| 4 | Hardening: screening, reports, bans, resets, privacy page, Turnstile | Done |
| 5 | **Operating model**: a team of agents with set jobs, briefings and audits on Telegram, documented | Done (exit test being watched) |
| 6 | **Native experience**: installable app (PWA), offline reading, push alerts for P0 notices, mobile and desktop polish | In progress (installable, offline, push and Events are live) |
| 7 | More sources: Halifax Water, Halifax Transit service alerts, Nova Scotia Health, more event calendars | Next |
| 8 | Off-site reach: social posts for P0 notices and the day's top stories, then a daily digest | Later |

The detailed plan, with what each phase must prove before the next, is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

Social media comes after the site is good on its own. Every post off-site points back to the story and its room, and
follows the same guidelines as the story itself.

## What success looks like

- A P0 notice is on the site within an hour of the source publishing it, with no wrong facts.
- Every developing story has been checked against its source in the last 12 hours.
- The editor spends minutes a day on Telegram, not hours.
- People open the site when something happens in Halifax, and stay to talk.
